About the Game

A factory game where everything is trying to kill you. Including your team.

Quiet Bruise is a chaotic factory survival game where dumb plushies build unstable production lines, fight ghost raids and somehow keep the whole thing from collapsing.

Machines catch fire, ghosts tear through production lines and your plushies barely know what they're doing. Build new machines, reroute broken systems, survive the night and pay off just enough debt to do it all again.

Every machine sounds wrong. Every plushie has terrible ideas. Keep it running anyway.

Simulation Strategy Roguelite Card Game Survival Economy 2D Hand-drawn Dark Humor Base Building Indie
