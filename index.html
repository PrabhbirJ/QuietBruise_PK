About the Game
A factory game where everything is trying to destroy it. Including the people running it.

Connect some belts, sell the output. Easy.

Then the raids started. A plushie shot a machine. The machine caught fire. Someone laid an egg in the middle of the factory. You fix what you can. You pay what you owe. You do it again.

Quiet Bruise is a chaotic factory survival roguelite. Build production lines, fight off raids, throw your teammates at enemies, and try not to destroy everything yourself.

You will destroy everything yourself.

Roguelite Factory Survival Chaos Plushies 2D Indie Singleplayer
  • 🪄
    Noita Wand. Brotato Bullets. Plushie Factory. The factory produces resources. The wand consumes them. What comes out is your problem.
  • 🥚
    Death Is A Resource Brood Knot: plushies drop eggs when they die. Eggs hatch. Those hatch too. One death can cascade into an army.
  • 🧠
    No Player Character. Every Plushie Is You. Body-hop anyone on the board at any time. The others keep running — Basil heals, Bruno charges, Duke buffs. The board never stops.
  • 🔥
    The Belt Is The Fuse Fire spreads along routing. Your production line isn't infrastructure — it's a countdown.
  • 📦
    Items Stack. Chaos Compounds. Risk of Rain 2–style global stacks on a board already on fire, already in debt, already mutating. The ceiling doesn't exist.
